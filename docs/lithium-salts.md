## Overview

The SCAN platform catalogs **13 lithium salts** across **11,515,140 non-aqueous electrolyte formulations**, covering an ionic-conductivity atlas generated by a multi-feature fusion network with dynamic routing. The model achieves a benchmark error of 0.372 mS/cm (65.4% reduction over prior models). LiFSI-, LiTFSI-, and LiBOB-based electrolytes have been validated to reach >20 mS/cm.
